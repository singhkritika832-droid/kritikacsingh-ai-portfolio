Work Notes About

I started in computer science and moved into design.

That mix still shapes how I work. I design the interface and the behaviour behind it, and I prototype in real code early so ideas meet reality fast.

For six years I've designed products from first sketch to launch: AI features for a video collaboration platform, a mobile app for construction sites, login and onboarding for one of India's largest banks, and enterprise portals for a US health insurer.

Lately most of my work sits where people and AI meet. I care about the moments where the system has to decide whether to act, ask, or wait, and I build my own agents with the Claude API to understand those moments from the inside.

I've mentored junior designers, and I like working closely with developers, sharing rough work early and refining it together.

I live in Dublin and I'm open to relocating.
