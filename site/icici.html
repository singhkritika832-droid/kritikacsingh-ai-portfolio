ICICI Bank

Designing a bank login around forgetting.

Role
UX Designer at Sparklin Innovation, for ICICI Bank
When
2022, three months
Platforms
Net banking on web and mobile
Worked with
Product, engineering, QA

Starting point

ICICI is India's largest private-sector bank, with 79M+ retail customers. Most of them open net banking only every few months, yet the login assumed they'd remember a User ID, get through one long form in a single go, and never time out. "Forgot User ID" was the biggest onboarding support ticket, and only 60% of people finished verification.

Outcome

A login with two ways in, recovery without leaving the screen, and verification split into steps that save as you go, all built on a new design system shared by web and mobile. Verification completion rose from 60% to 82%, onboarding got 25% faster, and time to first transaction fell 35%.

The form wasn't the problem. The whole flow assumed nothing would go wrong: no forgotten ID, no timeout, no interruption. For people who log in a few times a year, forgetting is the normal case, so I redesigned around recovery.

From one way in to a way back

I ran 20+ interviews and 7 heuristic reviews across web and mobile, and traced the funnel and support tickets. They pointed at the same moment: someone who can't remember their User ID has no way forward.

Before

  1. Enter User ID
  2. Can't remember it
  3. Call support, or give up

One long verification form. An error or timeout anywhere erased everything.

After

  1. Log in with User ID, or with your registered mobile number
  2. Forgot your ID? Get it right there, using your bank account, debit, credit or travel card
  3. Back in, without a phone call

Verification on separate screens. Each step saves, and you can see how much is left.

One decision per screen

Interviews named session timeouts as the biggest frustration. Losing everything read as the bank losing track of you. Splitting login into single screens meant an error or timeout only cost one step, and each screen asks for just one thing.

A design system underneath it all

Every heuristic review flagged web and mobile behaving differently, at exactly the moment people needed to trust the bank. So I built ICICI's design system from scratch: token architecture, 80+ components and documentation, held to WCAG 2.1 AA and adopted by other engineering teams.
